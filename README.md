# CRT TV Screensaver 

Just a fun little project inspired by the old DVD player / CRT TV bouncing-screen effect.

If your PC is running a background task and you want something cool to leave on the screen, just type your preferred text and let it float around. It has a simple old-school CRT TV style.

## How to use it?

Open the page.(https://md-althaf.github.io/Screen-saver/screen%20saver/index.html)
Clear the default text.

Type your preferred text.

Press Add.

Press F11 for fullscreen.

Leave it running and enjoy the old-school screensaver vibe! 

## Why did I make this?

I made it just for fun! 

Nothing too serious — I just wanted to recreate that nostalgic bouncing-screen effect from old DVD players and CRT TVs.

I have used AI in UI Style.css for the CRT look effect.
